System Capabilities

Core Portal Features

Standardized tools supporting student examinations and faculty assessment workflows.

📑

Flexible Quiz Management

Faculty can create and configure quizzes with dynamic sizing (5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40+ questions), assign subject units, and control publishing.

⏱️

Timed Online Examination

Server-enforced countdown timers synchronize examinations across candidate sessions and automatically submit when the allotted duration ends.

⚡

Automatic Evaluation

Every submission is evaluated immediately using verified answer keys, computing obtained marks, percentages, and institutional pass/fail status.

🏆

Instant Results & Review

Candidates receive immediate scorecards alongside question-by-question answer reviews and explanations, with downloadable official PDF scorecards.

📊

Student Performance Tracking

Complete attempt logs and historical scores are permanently preserved for each candidate by roll number and institutional division.

📈

Faculty Analytics

Deep visual analytics present real statistical distributions, curriculum unit-wise comparisons, pass/fail ratios, and class performance metrics.

🛡️

Secure Role-Based Access

Protected sessions isolate student examinations from faculty authoring, and server-side logic masks correct answer keys until post-submission.

Assessment Workflow

How the Portal Works

Clear, structured steps for students undertaking assessments and faculty managing examinations.

🎓

For Students

Examination Candidate Journey

  1. 1
    Login with your Roll Number, Full Name, and Division (A through Q).
  2. 2
    Select Quiz from available published assessments on your dashboard.
  3. 3
    Take Exam using the question palette with countdown timer tracking.
  4. 4
    Submit your responses manually or automatically when the timer reaches 00:00.
  5. 5
    View Result instantly with score breakdown, correct answers, and PDF download.
🛡️

For Faculty

Examination Authority Workflow

  1. 1
    Login with verified faculty institutional credentials.
  2. 2
    Create Quiz with custom durations, marks, and syllabus unit details.
  3. 3
    Add Questions to the question bank with options, answer keys, and explanations.
  4. 4
    Publish the assessment to make it accessible to eligible student divisions.
  5. 5
    Monitor Results, inspect analytics, track student profiles, and export CSV reports.
